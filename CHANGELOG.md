# Changelog

## v1.1.0
- **Handbook depth.** All eight lifecycle chapters rewritten to one template: decisions, KPIs, data and systems, classical methods, what fits, AI in practice in 2025 and 2026, a worked example, common mistakes, demos, papers, tools, open problems and further reading. Every statistic links to its source.
- **New reference docs.** [KPI dictionary](docs/reference/kpis.md), [data standards map](docs/reference/data-standards.md), [glossary](docs/glossary.md) grown from 18 to 140+ terms.
- **New playbooks.** [Agent architecture](docs/playbooks/agent-architecture.md), [evaluation](docs/playbooks/evaluation.md), [deploying AI](docs/playbooks/deploying-ai.md) and a [learning path](docs/learning-path.md). The [fit guide](docs/technology/fit-guide.md) gains a decision checklist and sourced verdicts.
- **Papers, tools, datasets.** Papers grew from 73 to 210+, with new sections for forecast evaluation, inventory control, transport and freight markets, manufacturing, documents, returns and digital twins. Every arXiv title and first-version year was checked against arXiv, and earlier titles that had changed were corrected. Tools grew to 70+ (inactive repos marked), datasets to 25 with licences where stated.
- **Six new demos (09 to 14).** Safety stock, dual sourcing, changeover scheduling, ETA calibration with conformal prediction, bill of lading validation, returns disposition. Every lifecycle stage now has a demo.
- **SCX-Bench v0.2.** 30 tasks (from 8), each with a trap; agents see observations, not simulator state; an optional LLM agent for Anthropic or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint; `--json` results for leaderboard submissions.
- **Repo.** Issue templates for problems and benchmark results; the link checker can be run by hand.
