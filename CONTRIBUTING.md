# Contributing

Thanks for helping. This repo stays useful only if every entry earns its place.

## What gets in
- **Papers:** 2025 or later (older only if foundational), with a link to the paper and one line on why a practitioner should read it. File it under the problem it solves.
- **Tools:** open source, maintained, and clearly tied to a supply chain problem.
- **Datasets:** public, with a clear licence.
- **Benchmark tasks:** add an entry to `benchmark/tasks.json` with a goal, allowed tools, money tools and a success condition. Include one trap (an action that looks helpful but is wrong).
- **Implementations:** runnable in under five minutes, under ~200 lines, with a README that says what to notice.

## What stays out
Vendor marketing, closed products, paywalled content without an open version, and anything you can't link to.

## How
1. Fork, add your entry in the right table, keep the table's format.
2. One entry per line, sorted by year then title.
3. Open a pull request with the title `Add: <name>`.

The link checker runs on every pull request.
